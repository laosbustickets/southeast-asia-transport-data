# Indonesia: transport routes, places and map

The data behind the [Indonesia transport map](https://indonesiatransport.com/indonesia-transport-map/) on
[IndonesiaTransport.com](https://indonesiatransport.com/), an independent guide in eight languages to trains, fast
boats, car ferries, buses and flights across Indonesia. The files here are copied every night from the live site, so
they always match what travellers read there.

## Files

| File | What it holds |
|---|---|
| `indonesia-transport-routes.csv` / `.json` | Train, car-ferry and fast-boat links between the places on the map: both ends with coordinates, the operators where known, the guide page that covers the link, the date it was checked and the source URLs |
| `indonesia-transit-places.csv` / `.json` / `.geojson` | Towns, harbours, stations and airports with their positions and IATA codes |
| `maps/indonesia-transport-map-<lang>.svg` | The transport map in English, French, German, Spanish, Japanese, Korean, Russian and Traditional Chinese, portrait and landscape |

## Where each link is explained

Every route row points to the guide page that covers it, with departures, fares for the month and where to board.
The busiest ones:

- [Bali to the Gili Islands](https://indonesiatransport.com/bali-to-gili-islands/) and [Bali to Lombok](https://indonesiatransport.com/bali-to-lombok/) by fast boat or car ferry from Padang Bai
- [Bali to Nusa Penida](https://indonesiatransport.com/bali-to-nusa-penida/) from Sanur
- [Jakarta to Bali overland](https://indonesiatransport.com/jakarta-to-bali/), with the Ketapang to Gilimanuk ferry
- [Jakarta to Yogyakarta](https://indonesiatransport.com/jakarta-to-yogyakarta/) and [Jakarta to Bandung](https://indonesiatransport.com/jakarta-to-bandung/) by train
- [Singapore to Batam](https://indonesiatransport.com/singapore-to-batam/) by ferry

Guides by way of travel: [trains](https://indonesiatransport.com/trains/), [ferries and fast boats](https://indonesiatransport.com/ferries/),
[buses](https://indonesiatransport.com/buses/) and [domestic flights](https://indonesiatransport.com/flights/). Entry rules
(visa on arrival, the arrival card and the Bali levy): [Indonesia visa guide](https://indonesiatransport.com/indonesia-visa/).
