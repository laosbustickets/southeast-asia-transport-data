Sources inside the data keep their own terms: Natural Earth, public domain (country outlines, places); GeoNames, CC BY 4.0; airport-codes / OurAirports, PDDL; border-crossing positions © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL (https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). The source of each place is listed in thailand-transport-map.json.

A coordinate marks an area, not a station or pier entrance, and a line is a corridor, not a timetable: neither says that a service runs or that a border is open.
